## Design Context

### Users
Whey4You serves both first-time supplement buyers who need plain-language guidance and experienced gym-goers who compare macros, serving count, price, flavour, and stock before buying. The primary job is to choose the correct product variant confidently and add it to the cart without losing technical product detail.

### Brand Personality
Confident, credible, and performance-focused. The Vietnamese voice should be direct and helpful, avoiding hype, fake urgency, and unverified health or certification claims.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a performance-editorial commerce direction: Apple's disciplined hierarchy and whitespace, Nike's product-led imagery and decisive purchase flow, and Myprotein's domain-specific nutrition clarity. Keep a light neutral canvas, dark typography, and Whey4You blue as a restrained action accent. Avoid excessive pills, gradients, glass effects, colored metric tiles, oversized radii, and decorative shadows.

### Implementation Defaults
Use the detected Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, custom UI primitives, and Supabase stack. Prefer React Server Components for product data loading, focused client components for variant selection and cart actions, typed service mappers at data boundaries, and existing utilities/components before adding new ones. No additional component, form, table, or animation library should be introduced for this redesign.

### Design Principles
1. Establish hierarchy with spacing, type, and alignment before using color or elevation.
2. Keep one unmistakable primary purchase action; secondary actions must remain visually quieter.
3. Show beginner-friendly summaries first and progressively disclose technical nutrition detail.
4. Every trust claim, price, stock state, image, and specification must come from real product data or be omitted.
5. Mobile purchase controls must remain reachable, resilient at 320px, and usable at 200% zoom.
